    Welcome to Gnosis

    Gnosis (γνῶσις) is the New Testament's Greek word for knowledge that comes from knowing God personally, as in “the surpassing worth of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord” (Philippians 3:8, NIV). Every prompt has many right answers, and the deeper the root, the more it's worth. Scoring follows Jesus' parables, from a Mustard Seed (10 Manna) up to the hundredfold harvest of Good Soil (100 Manna). This is the biblical word for knowing God, not the second-century Gnostic movement the early church rejected.

    What you're growing

    🌳 The tree
    You, planted by God. A tree by the rivers doesn't strain to grow; it simply drinks (Psalm 1:3).
    🌱 The roots
    Anchored in Jesus, drawing life from His unconditional love rather than your own effort. Keep feeding on His Word of grace, and your roots grow deeper on their own (Colossians 2:7).
    🍎 The fruit
    What grows from resting in Him. Branches don't labour to bear fruit; they stay joined to the Vine (John 15:5).
    🪟 The window
    His light on your life. Every answer lights a pane, and that light is what makes the tree grow (Psalm 119:105).
    ✨ The manna
    Fresh grace every morning. Jesus is the true bread from heaven, so there's a new round each day (John 6:35).

    Why time with the Lord and His Word matters

    We don't spend time with Him to earn His love. We spend time with Him because that's where we receive it. His Word is how we hear His voice, and our faith grows as we hear it (Romans 10:17). Like manna, yesterday's portion won't feed today, so come back each day and let Him fill you afresh.
